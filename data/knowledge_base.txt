15.1 GenAI Internship

The GenAI Internship is a multi-level learning program. Each level is structured around approximately 10 days. Daily learning can include reading material, quizzes or assessments, industry tips, CodeQuest coding challenges, and LeetPrompt prompt-engineering challenges. Some days are project-focused. Ask Dave is available on reading pages.

The supplied platform data describes five levels:
- Level 1 — GenAI Foundations: Generative AI fundamentals, prompt engineering, and Python basics.
- Level 2 — AI Systems & Engineering: Building structured AI applications with Python.
- Level 3 — Advanced Python & Production AI Systems: A production-ready AI recommendation-engine build.
- Level 4 — NeuroFlow Production AI Platform: A multimodal LLM orchestration platform covering RAG, evaluation, fine-tuning, and deployment.
- Level 5 — GenAI Systems Deep Dive: RAG, MCP, agent governance, evaluation strategy, system architecture, and PRD work.

A day may require completion of its linked CodeQuest and/or LeetPrompt challenge before Mark Complete becomes available. Some days also have project or GitHub requirements. The supplied platform guidance identifies a score of at least 50% as a possible challenge-completion threshold; the threshold may vary by day.

Main page: https://app.hidevs.xyz/genai-internship
